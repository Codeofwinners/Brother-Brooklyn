The Founder

"I built the tool I wish I had growing up."

Hi, I’m Rodney Coley. Most people know me as a retired aerospace engineer and roboticist. I spent years designing complex systems for the U.S. government and the private sector.

But my most important mission is this.

I saw too many young people—especially in our underserved communities—struggling not because they lacked potential, but because they lacked the proper mental programming. As a veteran and a father, I knew I had to do something.

I created Inner Man Education and BrainBot to bridge that gap. To take high-level neuroscience out of the lab and put it directly into your hands. I want you to have the tools to define your own future.
