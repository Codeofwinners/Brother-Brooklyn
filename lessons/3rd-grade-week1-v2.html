Decoding the Blueprint

🤖

"A blueprint is a map of someone's dream. If you can read it, you can build anything!"

The Texture Lab

🤖

"In life, you're gonna hit resistance. In science, we call it Friction. We don't get mad -- we measure it!"

Time for a real experiment! You'll test how surfaces affect a rolling car. Let's get scientific!

rocket_launch What You'll Do

1

Roll a toy car down a ramp

Roll it onto 3 different surfaces: carpet, tile, and sandpaper.

2

Measure how far it goes

Use a ruler or your hand to measure the distance each time.

3

Figure out: Why does sandpaper stop the car?

The answer is friction! Rough surfaces create more friction.

📏

Achievement Unlocked

The Friction Table

Record your distance data for each surface.
