Day 1 — Monday
1 Monday

"Meet Your Thinker Brain!"

flag Student Objectives

  • check_circle Identify the front part of the brain (PFC)
  • check_circle Participate in the "Wake Up Thinker Brain" routine

"The PFC is the center for focus, choices, and impulse control. Pre-K kids must often 'borrow' the adult's PFC."

inventory_2 Materials Needed

check_box_outline_blank Wimee Brain City intro video
check_box_outline_blank Hand mirror for each student
check_box_outline_blank Color dot sticker (for "Thinker Brain")
check_box_outline_blank Printed outline of a head

route Lesson Flow

1
Greeting Ritual

"Hi friends, Wimee here! Your brain is AMAZING!"

2
Brain Activation

Tap forehead: "Wake up, Thinker Brain!"

3
Mini-Lesson

Teacher shows mirror: "Point to where your brain helps you make choices."

4
Video

Wimee Brain City Intro (45 sec)

5
Hands-On

Students place the sticker on the PFC on a head outline

6
SEL Check-In

"Is your Thinker Brain calm, excited, or sleepy today?"

assignment_turned_in
Assessment

Student points to PFC

Day 2 — Tuesday
2 Tuesday

"Stop, Think, Choose!"

flag Student Objectives

  • check_circle Practice using the three-step Thinker Brain plan
  • check_circle Understand "stopping" before doing

"Teach PFC sequencing (stop → think → choose) to strengthen neural pathways. Use slow modeling; children copy your pacing."

inventory_2 Materials Needed

check_box_outline_blank 3 large cards: STOP / THINK / CHOOSE
check_box_outline_blank Red, yellow, green circles
check_box_outline_blank Classroom scenario pictures

route Lesson Flow

1
Wimee Greeting

"Let's use our Thinker Brain to plan our day!"

2
Brain Ritual

"Tap 1-2-3: Stop, Think, Choose!"

3
Mini-Lesson

Teacher holds cards and models: "STOP... (pause) THINK... (pause) CHOOSE calm hands."

4
Scenario Activity

Students sort pictures into what Wimee would STOP, THINK, or CHOOSE

5
Movement Game

Red circle = Freeze, Yellow = Slow motion, Green = Gentle go

assignment_turned_in
Assessment

Students perform a full STC sequence on their own

Day 3 — Wednesday
3 Wednesday

"My Feelings and My Brain"

flag Student Objectives

  • check_circle Identify feelings using simple body cues
  • check_circle Understand the PFC helps calm big feelings

"You are teaching interoception (body awareness). Naming feelings reduces amygdala activation."

inventory_2 Materials Needed

check_box_outline_blank Feeling face cards
check_box_outline_blank "Where I feel feelings in my body" handouts
check_box_outline_blank Calm-down glitter jar or snow globe

route Lesson Flow

1
Wimee Greeting

"Your feelings are messages!"

2
Brain Ritual

"Hand on heart, hand on belly... breathe."

3
Mini-Lesson

"When your feelings are BIG, your Thinker Brain needs help."

4
Body Scan Activity

Point to belly for nervous, chest for excited, head for calm

5
Calm-Down Demonstration

Shake glitter jar; watch it settle. "This is your brain settling."

assignment_turned_in
Assessment

Students point to where they feel calm

Day 4 — Thursday
4 Thursday

"Brain City Safety Helpers"

flag Student Objectives

  • check_circle Apply the "Thinker Brain" to classroom safety
  • check_circle Understand the connection between choices and safety

"Today teaches executive function + situational awareness. Children learn safety by imitation first, reasoning second."

inventory_2 Materials Needed

check_box_outline_blank Classroom Safety Picture Cards
check_box_outline_blank Floor dots / tape
check_box_outline_blank Wimee "Safety Helper" printable badge

route Lesson Flow

1
Wimee Greeting

"Let's use your Thinker Brain to stay safe!"

2
PFC Ritual

Forehead tap + affirmation: "I can choose safety."

3
Mini-Lesson

Compare "fast running brain" vs "Thinker Brain walking feet."

4
Safety Stations

Walking path practice, Safe hands practice, Listening ears practice

5
Safety Helper Badges

Students earn temporary badges for showing safe choices

assignment_turned_in
Assessment

Students model 3 safe choices

Day 5 — Friday
5 Friday

"Wimee's Thinker Brain Celebration!"

flag Student Objectives

  • check_circle Review everything learned about the PFC
  • check_circle Demonstrate using their Thinker Brain in a group

"Reinforcement builds myelination. Repetition = stronger neural pathways."

inventory_2 Materials Needed

check_box_outline_blank Wimee celebration song video
check_box_outline_blank Brain City coloring page
check_box_outline_blank Stickers & stamps
check_box_outline_blank Thinker Brain reflection sheet

route Lesson Flow

1
Wimee Greeting

"We are Thinker Brain Experts!"

2
Brain Ritual Combo

Tap forehead, deep breath, say affirmation

3
Mini Lesson

Review the week using 5 picture cards

4
Thinker Brain Showcase

Students share 1 time they "Stopped, Thought, Chose"

5
Art Activity

Color Brain City, add a star sticker on the PFC
