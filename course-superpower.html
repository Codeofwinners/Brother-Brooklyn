01 Lesson 1

The Survival Home

psychology
warning

The Symptom

Living in high-pressure environments means the whole family might be soaked in Cortisol. This looks like short tempers, constant arguing, or shutting down (the Freeze).

psychology
Neuro-Truth

It's not attitude.
It's Hypervigilance.

When the brain feels unsafe outside, it struggles to turn the "Alarm" (the Amygdala) off inside. Don't take it personally — it's a chemical spike.

lightbulb

Key Takeaway

Your child's defiance might actually be their brain stuck in survival mode. Recognizing the chemical response is the first step to changing it.
