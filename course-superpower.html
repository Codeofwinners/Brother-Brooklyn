01 Lesson 1

Why Does My Brain Bug Out?

psychology
warning

Your Brain Has a Smoke Alarm

Deep inside your brain there's a tiny part called the Amygdala. Think of it like a smoke alarm. Its job? To keep you safe. When it senses danger — real or imagined — it pulls the alarm and floods your body with a stress chemical called Cortisol.

That's why your heart races before a test, your stomach drops when someone yells, or you freeze up when you get called on in class. It's not your fault. It's your brain trying to protect you.

psychology
Fight, Flight, or Freeze

Which One Are You?

🥊

Fight

You get angry, argue, or lash out. An argument with a friend and you're ready to go off.

🏃

Flight

You want to escape — skip class, put your hood up, zone out on your phone.

🧊

Freeze

You shut down. Teacher calls on you and your mind goes blank. You can't move, can't speak.

lightbulb

The Big Idea

Your brain isn't broken — it's just stuck in survival mode. Once you understand that, you can learn to control it. Walking through a rough neighborhood, getting into it with a friend, presenting in front of class — your alarm goes off every time. But here's the thing: you can learn to turn it down.

02 Lesson 2

The Copycat Effect

people
people

Your Brain is a Copycat

Your brain has something called Mirror Neurons. They're always watching, always copying. If everyone around you is stressed, angry, or giving up — your brain starts copying that energy.

But here's the flip side: it works BOTH ways. Surround yourself with people who are growing, learning, and staying calm — and your brain copies THAT instead.

Real Talk

"You become the average of the 5 people you spend the most time with — neurologically. Your brain literally rewires itself based on who's around you."

The Big Idea

Your mirror neurons don't have a filter — they copy everything. The good news? You get to choose what you feed them. Change your circle, change your brain.

03 Lesson 3

Hack Your Brain Chemistry

You are the pharmacist of your own brain. Here's your prescription.

bolt

Dopamine

The Reward Chemical

Celebrate small wins. Finished your homework? That counts. Walked away from drama? Championship move. Your brain gives you a hit of dopamine every time you win — even small ones.

emoji_events

Serotonin

The Confidence Chemical

Help someone out. Stand tall — literally. Your body language changes your brain chemistry. When you help a classmate or hold the door, serotonin flows.

favorite

Oxytocin

The Trust Chemical

Real connections beat social media likes every time. A genuine conversation, a real laugh with your crew — that's oxytocin. Your brain craves real bonds, not notifications.

directions_run

Endorphins

The Painkiller

Exercise, laugh, create something. Draw, play ball, make music. Endorphins are your brain's natural painkiller — and they're free.

Your Daily Prescription

D

Celebrate one small win today — even getting out of bed counts

S

Help one person — hold the door, share notes, say something kind

O

Have one real conversation — put the phone down, look someone in the eye

E

Move your body — walk, run, dance, shoot hoops. Even 10 minutes works.

lightbulb

The Big Idea

You are the pharmacist of your own brain. You don't need a prescription — you need a plan. Every day you get to choose which chemicals you release. That's your superpower.
