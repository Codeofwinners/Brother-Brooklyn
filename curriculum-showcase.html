Teacher Support

Day 1 Teacher Toolkit

Every teacher receives a complete, ready-to-use toolkit for their first day. No guesswork. No gaps.

1

Welcome Script

A ready-to-read opening script that sets classroom tone, introduces brain science framing, and creates a safe, exciting first impression. Customized for each grade band — from Wimee's playful voice to BroBot's mentor tone.

2

Visual Anchor

Grade-appropriate brain poster or digital display. PreK-2 gets the Brain City Map; 3-5 gets the Brain Roads poster; 6-12 gets the 'Your Brain Is a Supercomputer' display.

3

Classroom Ritual

A repeatable 3-minute opening routine: call-and-response affirmation, breathing reset, and the 'Check My Brain' hand signal students use daily.

4

Materials List

Complete supply list for hands-on activities, pre-sorted by quarter. Includes student reflection sheets, brain cards, coding tiles, and BroBot QR codes.

5

Mini-Lesson Script

Word-for-word 5-minute brain science lesson script with discussion prompts. Covers the PFC, RAS filters, and emotional triggers in age-appropriate language.

6

Model Scenario

A pre-written culturally responsive scenario for guided practice. Students identify which brain part is active, what emotions show up, and what a wise future-self choice looks like.

7

Day 1 Assignment

Structured first activity with differentiation notes for diverse learners: Draw your Brain City, write your 'Future Me' statement, and set one small habit. Includes rubric and family extension activity.

8

Teacher Notes

Trauma-informed tips, cultural sensitivity guidance, classroom management strategies, and protocols for speaking respectfully to students in crisis. Because Day 1 sets the tone for the entire year.
